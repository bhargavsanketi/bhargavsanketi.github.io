Bhargav D Sanketi, PhD

How tissues build themselves. How they break. How to rewrite them.

I study tissue state. Tissues are not fixed by their genes. They move between states to get things done.

Those shifts run on mechanics, signaling, transcription, metabolism, and chromatin. They set whether a tissue builds itself up or breaks itself down. Body text 19px, line height 1.6, measure capped at 66 characters.

Building tissues

I asked how the gut gets its shape.

We built tools to measure and move mechanical forces inside a living embryo. We found that rotation starts when local mechanical feedback amplifies the body plan.

Developmental programs are modular. They get reused far beyond the organ that first deploys them.
